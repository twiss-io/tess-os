# Cyra security verdict: PR #198 (v0.2.0 train: ten-role roster plus lens library)

Signed contract: the YAML front-matter above. The signature covers only the front-matter. This body is the human-readable record.

## Threat model
Three adversaries. First, a prompt-injection author whose text reaches a dispatched role and tries to make a review role write, or to widen a role's tools. Second, an adopter-side accident or a hostile file in .codex/agents/ (symlink, spoofed header) that tries to make the renderer write or delete outside that directory. Third, a contributor who uses a large roster rewrite to quietly weaken a guardrail, a gate or the verifier policy.

## Attack surface
The new Codex custom-agent render (.tess/bin/tessctl:2259-2370), its bench/recruit resync hooks, the .codex/agents/** owned_globs entry in tess.manifest.json, the nine role files and their tool lists, and the protected doctrine files guardrails.md, channel-guardrails.md and verification-routing.md.

## What changed, security-wise
- Write/delete containment holds. Every write and removal goes through the C1 manifest gate. Reproduced: a symlinked toml is skipped; a symlinked .codex/agents directory aborts the render before any write or delete.
- Sandbox fails closed. Only an exact `sandbox: workspace-write` yields workspace-write.
- Least privilege holds. Review and explorer roles have no Write/Edit.
- Doctrine and policy lose nothing. No gate, rule or hard floor was loosened, and the verifier allowlists and schemas are unchanged.

## Residual risk
Four LOW findings, none blocking: a role-name collision overwrites a user toml; header-prefix removal can take a user's edited copy of a generated file; read-only for Bash-holding roles is prompt-enforced (documented); roster.md's Codex model advice points at a generated file. Agent-held custody of the Cyra key remains the known v0.2.1 item.
