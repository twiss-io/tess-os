## Threat model
The adversary is a prompt-injected or misaligned agent session, or a malicious contributor. It has write access to the working tree, and it reads shipped doctrine as instructions. Its goals:
- use doctrine to relax Rule Zero, the incident-ops exception, client isolation or the Rule 18 hard floor;
- make the operator believe a guard enforces something it does not;
- get private overlay data (kb, clients) committed to a shared or public remote.

## What changed, security-wise
